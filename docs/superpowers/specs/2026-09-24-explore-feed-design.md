# Explore feed (direction F) + 24-hour owner posts

> **Revised 2026-09-24 (same day):** the separate "גלו" feed tab was removed at the user's request.
> Owner posts now appear as **Instagram-style story circles at the top of the home page**
> (`customer/StoriesBar.tsx`, `stories.ts`, `stories.css`): one circle per store with live posts,
> a brand-coloured ring until this device has seen them, and a full-screen viewer (6 s per photo,
> tap/arrow keys to step, press or pause button to hold, Escape or swipe down to close, linked dish
> orders through `ProductSheet`). `/explore` now redirects to `/`. The owner page, callables, rules,
> sweep and data model below are unchanged. The feed sections below are historical.

Date: 2026-09-24. Source: mockup F in `docs/mockups/landing-2026-09-24/landing-directions.html`,
chosen by the user, plus their addition: owners post photos that stay visible for 24 hours and appear
in the feed next to the dishes.

## What the customer gets

- A new bottom-nav tab, **גלו** (`/explore`), next to ראשי. The home page stays as it is.
- A full-screen vertical feed with scroll snapping: one card per screen, swipe (or wheel / arrow keys)
  to the next.
- Two kinds of cards, interleaved (a post, then two dishes, repeated; posts newest first):
  - **Dish**: a photographed, available product of a restaurant that serves the chosen city. Photo,
    store name + open/closed state, dish name, short description, price, "Add to cart" (opens the
    existing `ProductSheet`, so variants, extras, cart-from-another-store and checkout all behave as on
    the store page), heart (product favorite), store button (`/b/:business/:branch`), share.
  - **Post**: a photo an owner published in the last 24 hours, with an optional caption and an optional
    linked dish. Shows "N hours ago". Linked dish → "Order" opens the product sheet; otherwise "Menu".
- Dishes from open stores come first; within a store, "most ordered" first; stores are round-robined
  so no single store fills the feed. Closed / paused / not-serving-this-city stores keep their cards
  but the add button is replaced by the reason.
- Empty city → empty state with "change town". End of feed → "You've seen everything" card.

## What the owner gets

- A new business-panel page, **סטוריז** (`/business/:b/:br/posts`, sidebar "Menu" section and the
  mobile "More" page), for owners and managers (`catalog` permission).
- "New post": pick/take a photo, optional caption, optional linked dish, **Publish for 24 hours**.
- List of live posts with time left and delete.

## Data

`BranchPost` in `@qareeb/shared`:
`{ id, businessId, branchId, imagePath, caption: Localized, productId?, createdAt, expiresAt }`
(`expiresAt` = publish time + 24h, ISO).

- Private: `businesses/{b}/branches/{br}/posts/{id}`; public projection:
  `publicBranches/{br}/posts/{id}` (only while the branch is publicly visible), read by anyone.
- Images: `businesses/{b}/branches/{br}/posts/{id}/{file}` (same Storage rule shape as promotions;
  the resize trigger makes the usual thumb/display variants).
- Callables (catalog roles, branch-scoped):
  - `publishPost({ businessId, branchId, postId, path, caption, productId? })`: postId is generated by
    the client so the photo can be uploaded first; validates the path prefix, the uploaded object
    (type/size), the linked product (live product of the branch), the cap (`MAX_LIVE_POSTS` = 10 live
    per branch); writes doc + projection + audit.
  - `removePost({ businessId, branchId, postId })`: deletes doc, projection and image.
- Expiry: the client queries `expiresAt > now` and filters again on render; `scheduledSweeps` deletes
  expired posts (private + public docs and images) via a collection-group query on `posts.expiresAt`
  (collection-group single-field index added to `firestore.indexes.json`). `reprojectCatalog`
  republishes/removes posts like promotions.

## Feed query (client)

`useExploreFeed(cityId)`: the branches from the existing discovery queries (both kinds, for posts;
restaurants only, for dishes), then one-shot reads per branch (max 20 branches):
`publicBranches/{id}/products` (`orderBy sortOrder`, limit 60, filtered to photographed + available)
and `publicBranches/{id}/posts` (`where expiresAt > now`). No cross-store collection-group query, so no
new rules or composite indexes on the customer path.
